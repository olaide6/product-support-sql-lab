Inactive Subscription Investigation

Scenario

The Customer Success team requested a list of customers whose subscriptions are inactive so they can begin renewal outreach.

Objective

Identify customers with inactive subscriptions.

Investigation

The subscriptions table was joined with the customers table to retrieve customer details where the subscription status was marked as "Inactive".

Findings

Three customers currently have inactive subscriptions and may require renewal follow-up.

Recommendation

Share the list with the Customer Success team so they can contact customers and reduce churn.
